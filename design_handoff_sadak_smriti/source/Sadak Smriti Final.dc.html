Sita Kumari Yadav

Her story · written by her husband, Ramesh

Sita taught Nepali and social studies at Shree Janata Secondary School in Lahan for eleven years. Her students called her Sita Miss, even after they left school and came back with children of their own.

On Saturdays she opened the school library for anyone who wanted to read. She sang Maithili folk songs while she cooked, grew chillies and marigolds on the roof, and never missed Chhath at her mother’s home.

She was on her way back from a teachers’ training in Janakpur. She leaves her husband, a son of twelve and a daughter of eight.
